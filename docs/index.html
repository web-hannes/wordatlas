      How to play

      1. Read the map. Nearby words mean similar things. The bold word is the map's theme.
      2. Drop your pin where the hidden word belongs. Drag or tap again to move it.
      3. Confirm to see the real spot. The closer you are, the more points, up to 1,000 a round.

      Five rounds a day, the same for everyone. A new map arrives at midnight UTC.
